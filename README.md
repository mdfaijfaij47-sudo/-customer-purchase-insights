# Customer Purchase Insights Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes customer buying behavior using Microsoft Excel to identify customer segments, purchasing patterns, and factors associated with bike purchases.

The analysis covers 1,027 customer records and examines customer demographics, income, occupation, region, commute distance, and purchase behavior to identify high-potential customer segments and develop targeted marketing recommendations.
## Business Objective

The objective of this analysis is to understand:

- Which customer segments have the highest purchase rates?
- Which age groups are more likely to purchase?
- Which occupations show stronger purchase behavior?
- Which regions have higher purchase rates?
- How does commute distance affect purchasing behavior?
- Does income influence purchasing behavior?
- How do marital status and car ownership relate to purchases?
- Which customer segments should be targeted by marketing campaigns?

## Key Business Insights

- Middle-aged customers showed stronger bike purchase behavior.
- Professionals represented one of the strongest customer segments
  based on purchase behavior.
- Customers from the Pacific region showed a higher purchase rate.
- Customers with a commute distance of 0–1 miles showed strong
  conversion behavior.
- Customer income, marital status, car ownership, and commute
  distance provided useful dimensions for customer segmentation.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the business can:

- Target middle-aged customers with personalized marketing campaigns.
- Focus campaigns on professional customer segments.
- Prioritize high-performing regions such as the Pacific region.
- Develop targeted offers for customers with shorter commute distances.
- Use income and demographic information to create differentiated
  customer segments.
- Build targeted marketing campaigns instead of using the same
  strategy for all customers.
